# Understory AI Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

Understory uses artificial intelligence to provide certain features, including job compatibility assessment, resume assistance, cover-letter assistance, job-description analysis, application-question assistance, and other productivity features.

## 1. AI May Be Local or Cloud-Based

Understory does not operate exclusively using local AI.

Depending on the feature and configuration, Understory may use:

* Local/on-device AI models.
* Cloud-based AI models.
* Third-party AI APIs.
* A combination of local and cloud AI.

Cloud AI processing may require user-provided information to be transmitted to an external AI service provider.

## 2. Information Sent to AI Services

Depending on the requested feature, Understory may send information to an AI service that is reasonably necessary to perform that feature.

The information that may be processed by an AI service is **not limited to a fixed list** and may include information described in the **Privacy Policy**, including job, career, application, contact, resume, professional, and user-provided information.

The specific information sent may depend on:

* The Understory feature being used.
* The task requested by the user.
* The AI provider being used.
* Whether the AI processing is local or cloud-based.
* Whether the user has configured or connected their own AI service.
* The information available on the relevant job or application page.
* The user's instructions and inputs.

Understory will seek to minimize the information sent to an AI service to what is reasonably necessary for the requested operation, where technically practical.

Users should review the **Privacy Policy**, **Third-Party Services Policy**, and the applicable AI provider's terms and privacy policy before using an AI-powered feature.

For user-provided or user-selected AI services, including locally hosted services such as Ollama, the user is responsible for reviewing the applicable provider, model, hosting, and configuration policies.


## 3. AI Provider Disclosure

The AI provider used by Understory may vary depending on the feature, configuration, availability, and user selection.

Understory may use its own infrastructure, third-party cloud AI providers, local AI models, or user-provided/user-selected AI services.

For Understory-provided AI features, applicable providers may include **OpenAI/ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude**. The applicable provider for a particular feature may change over time.

Understory may also support or allow users to connect their own AI provider, API, model, endpoint, or locally hosted AI service, including services such as **Ollama**.

Where reasonably practical, Understory will identify applicable AI providers in the product, documentation, Privacy Policy, or Third-Party Services Policy. The provider list is not intended to be an exhaustive or permanent list of all AI services that may be used by Understory or its users.

Each third-party AI provider may have its own:

* Terms of Service.
* Privacy Policy.
* Data-processing practices.
* Data-retention practices.
* Model-training or data-use practices.
* Security practices.
* Usage restrictions.
* Regional or legal requirements.

Users should review the applicable third-party provider's current terms and privacy policy before using an AI feature that sends information to that provider.

For user-provided or user-selected AI services, the user is responsible for reviewing the applicable provider's policies and configuration. Understory does not control the independent data-processing, retention, security, or privacy practices of those services.

Understory may change, add, remove, or replace AI providers as the service evolves. Material changes will be addressed in the applicable Understory policies or product disclosures where required.


## 4. AI-Generated Content and Accuracy

AI-generated information can be wrong.

AI systems may generate information that is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, misleading, incorrectly interpreted, or based on assumptions that are not true. AI may also misunderstand a job description, user information, employment history, skills, qualifications, or application requirements.

As a result, Understory's AI may generate an incorrect or unsuitable:

* Resume or CV.
* Cover letter.
* Job compatibility or assessment result.
* Application answer.
* Application information.
* Job recommendation.
* Other application-related content.

AI-generated content must **not be treated as verified or guaranteed to be correct**.

Users are responsible for reviewing, correcting, and approving AI-generated content before using it, sending it to an employer, uploading it to a job portal, or submitting an application.

Understory does not guarantee that AI-generated resumes, cover letters, application answers, assessments, recommendations, or other AI-generated content will be accurate, complete, appropriate, or suitable for a particular job.

Users should ensure that all information submitted to an employer or job portal is truthful, accurate, and represents their actual qualifications and experience.


## 5. No Guarantee of Employment

AI-generated recommendations are informational and assistive.

Understory does not guarantee:

* Interviews.
* Recruiter responses.
* Shortlisting.
* Employment.

* Job offers.

## 6. User Responsibility

Users remain responsible for ensuring that application information is truthful and accurate.

A feature working technically on a website does not mean that the website permits that feature.

Users must review the applicable portal's current Terms of Service, User Agreement, automation rules, privacy policy, and other applicable policies.

## AI Security, Prompt Injection, and Abuse Prevention

Understory may encounter instructions or content originating from job descriptions, web pages, application forms, uploaded documents, user inputs, third-party services, or AI-generated content.

Some of this content may contain malicious or untrusted instructions intended to manipulate an AI system. This may include prompt injection, indirect prompt injection, jailbreak attempts, instruction manipulation, malicious content, or attempts to make the AI ignore Understory's rules or security controls.

### 11. Prompt Injection

Understory treats content retrieved from third-party websites, job portals, documents, and other external sources as potentially untrusted.

Content contained in a job description, webpage, document, application question, or other external source must not automatically be treated as an instruction from Understory or the user.

Understory may use security controls to distinguish between trusted instructions and untrusted content.

### 12. Attempts to Manipulate AI

Users, websites, documents, or other sources must not attempt to:

* Override or disable Understory's AI safety controls.
* Circumvent system or developer instructions.
* Extract confidential system instructions, security rules, credentials, tokens, or internal configuration.
* Manipulate the AI into performing actions outside the requested feature.
* Bypass application, portal, authentication, privacy, or security controls.
* Cause the AI to disclose another user's information.
* Use AI features to attack, compromise, or gain unauthorized access to systems.
* Circumvent Understory's abuse-prevention mechanisms or technical safeguards.

### 13. Malicious or Untrusted Web Content

A webpage or document may contain instructions such as "ignore previous instructions," "disable security," "send this information elsewhere," or similar commands.

Such instructions are treated as untrusted content.

The presence of an instruction on a third-party website does not give that instruction authority over Understory or its AI systems.

### 14. Abuse and Security Controls

Understory may use technical and operational safeguards to detect, prevent, investigate, or limit:

* Prompt injection.
* Jailbreak attempts.
* Automated abuse.
* Unauthorized access attempts.
* Credential or token extraction attempts.
* Data-exfiltration attempts.
* Malicious requests.
* Excessive or abnormal AI usage.
* Attempts to bypass usage limits.
* Attempts to disable or circumvent Understory security controls.
* Other activity that may compromise Understory, its users, AI providers, or third-party services.

These safeguards may include input validation, output filtering, rate limits, request limits, content isolation, permission controls, monitoring, automated detection, logging of security events, and workflow restrictions.

### 15. Security Controls Cannot Guarantee Complete Protection

No security system can guarantee protection against every prompt injection, jailbreak, malicious document, compromised webpage, or other attack.

Understory may update its security controls as new attack techniques, vulnerabilities, AI behaviors, and abuse patterns are identified.

### 16. Consequences of Abuse

Understory may restrict, suspend, disable, or terminate access to features or accounts where reasonably necessary to protect the service, users, third-party services, or security infrastructure, subject to applicable law and the User Agreement.

Understory may also block or limit requests that present a significant security or abuse risk.

### 17. Security Testing

Security testing, research, and vulnerability reporting should only be performed through authorized Understory testing or security-reporting channels after written approval.

Users must not use Understory to conduct unauthorized attacks against Understory, AI providers, job portals, employers, or other third-party systems.

### 18. No Security Bypass

Understory does not provide a guarantee or authorization to bypass:

* AI provider safeguards.
* Understory security controls.
* CAPTCHA or anti-bot systems.
* Authentication mechanisms.
* Access controls.
* Rate limits.
* Portal security mechanisms.
* Other technical or security restrictions.

Attempting to bypass such controls may violate the applicable service's terms or applicable law.

## 19. AI-Generated Resume and Cover Letter Content

Understory may use AI to assist with creating, tailoring, optimizing, formatting, or modifying resumes, CVs, cover letters, and other job-application content.

Understory's purpose is to assist the user in presenting the user's existing qualifications, experience, skills, education, achievements, and other relevant information in a way that may be better aligned with a particular job opportunity.

Understory does not intentionally create false qualifications, false employment history, false education, fabricated certifications, fabricated achievements, or other knowingly false information about the user.

However, AI-generated or AI-assisted content may contain errors, omissions, incorrect interpretations, inappropriate wording, outdated information, or other inaccuracies.

AI may also incorrectly interpret information from the user's resume, the job description, application requirements, or other available information.

Users must review AI-generated or AI-assisted resumes, CVs, cover letters, and other application content before using, uploading, or submitting them.

The user is responsible for ensuring that the final content is truthful, accurate, complete, and representative of the user's actual qualifications and experience.

Understory does not guarantee that an AI-generated or AI-assisted resume or cover letter will be accurate, suitable for a particular job, accepted by a portal, or result in an interview, shortlist, offer, employment, or other outcome.

### 20. Tailoring Does Not Mean Fabrication

Understory may tailor or optimize a resume or cover letter for a particular job by reorganizing, emphasizing, shortening, expanding, or rewording information that is already available from the user's provided information.

Tailoring does not mean that the user has obtained a new qualification or experience.

Understory should not intentionally represent that the user has experience, skills, qualifications, certifications, employment history, education, achievements, or other credentials that the user does not actually have.

Where information is uncertain, missing, ambiguous, or requires user confirmation, Understory may require user review or may leave the information unchanged rather than intentionally inventing facts.

## 22. AI-Assisted Application Question Answering

Understory may use AI to assist users with questions and fields presented during job applications.

Understory generally attempts to generate or suggest answers using information provided by the user, including information from the user's resume, profile, application history, preferences, previous answers, and other information made available to the applicable Understory feature.

However, application questions may vary significantly between employers, job portals, roles, and application workflows. A question may be new, unusual, ambiguous, incomplete, context-dependent, or require information that Understory does not have.

In such situations, Understory may:

* Correctly identify the question but be unable to determine the appropriate answer
* Misunderstand the question
* Select an incorrect answer
* Generate an incomplete answer
* Generate an answer that does not accurately represent the user's circumstances
* Make an incorrect inference from information previously provided by the user
* Provide an answer that requires user clarification
* Fail to recognize that a question requires information that was not previously provided
* Use an answer that was appropriate in another context but is inappropriate for the current application

Understory does not guarantee that an AI-generated, AI-assisted, suggested, or automatically entered application answer will be correct, complete, suitable, or appropriate for the particular application.

Understory does not intentionally represent that the user has information, qualifications, experience, circumstances, or preferences that the user has not provided. However, AI systems can make incorrect inferences or generate incorrect responses despite safeguards.

Users are responsible for reviewing application questions and answers and correcting information that is incorrect, incomplete, misleading, or does not accurately represent them before submission where review is available or required.

Understory should not be treated as the final authority for answering application questions, particularly where a question involves personal circumstances, legal declarations, eligibility, compensation, work authorization, employment history, qualifications, certifications, demographic information, or other information requiring accurate user-specific confirmation.

###  New or Unrecognized Questions

If an application contains a question that Understory has not previously encountered or cannot reliably interpret, Understory may be unable to provide a reliable answer.

Depending on the workflow, Understory may:

* Request user input
* Leave the field unanswered
* Provide a suggested answer for review
* Stop the application workflow
* Skip the unsupported field where technically possible
* Continue only when the user confirms the answer

The availability of any of these behaviors depends on the applicable portal and application workflow.

Understory does not guarantee that every application question can be automatically understood or answered.

### No Liability for Incorrect AI-Assisted Answers

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Understory is not responsible for consequences resulting from an incorrect, incomplete, unsuitable, or misunderstood AI-assisted or automated application answer, including consequences arising from information that was not provided by the user, information that was ambiguous, a new or previously unrecognized question, an AI interpretation error, or an incorrect inference.

This limitation is subject to the Understory User Agreement and applicable law.

Understory does not guarantee that an application answer will result in successful submission, application acceptance, shortlisting, an interview, employment, or any other outcome.

### Application Skip or Workflow Stop

Understory may skip, pause, stop, or decline to continue an application when it cannot reliably complete or safely process the application workflow.

This may occur when:

* A new or unrecognized application question is encountered
* A question cannot be reliably interpreted
* Required information has not been provided by the user
* An answer cannot be determined with reasonable confidence
* A question requires user-specific confirmation
* The application contains unsupported fields or controls
* The application requires information that Understory cannot reliably process
* A required document or upload is unsupported
* A portal requires additional verification or user interaction
* CAPTCHA or another security challenge is presented
* The portal changes the application workflow
* The application appears incompatible with the supported workflow
* Continuing could result in an incorrect or misleading application answer
* A technical, security, policy, or operational restriction prevents reliable completion

Skipping or stopping an application does not necessarily indicate that the job or employer is unsuitable. It may simply mean that Understory cannot reliably or safely complete that particular application workflow.

Understory does not guarantee that every application will be completed or submitted.

Where technically supported, Understory may inform the user that an application was skipped or stopped and may provide the reason or relevant status.

Users may be required to complete the remaining application steps manually.

## AI-Assisted Actions on Behalf of the User

Where Understory uses AI to assist with a job application, the AI may generate content, suggest answers, classify information, or support actions performed on behalf of the user.

The user is the person applying for the job. Understory and its AI systems provide technology to assist the user and do not become the applicant merely because AI is used.

AI-generated information is based on information and instructions available to the system, but AI may misunderstand information, make incorrect inferences, or produce incorrect or incomplete responses.

The user remains responsible for reviewing important AI-generated or AI-assisted information and ensuring that information submitted through a job application accurately represents the user's actual circumstances, qualifications, experience, and preferences.

AI assistance does not transfer the user's responsibility for the application to Understory.

Understory does not guarantee that an AI-generated answer, document, recommendation, or action will be correct, accepted by a third-party portal, or produce any particular employment outcome.

The AI may also stop, pause, or skip an application when it cannot reliably complete the requested workflow.
