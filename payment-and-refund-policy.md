If Understory offers recurring subscriptions, the applicable billing frequency and recurring amount will be displayed before purchase.

Users may cancel a subscription through the cancellation method provided by Understory or the applicable payment platform.

Cancellation normally prevents future billing.

Cancellation does not automatically mean that previously paid amounts will be refunded.

Access to paid features may continue until the end of the applicable paid period, depending on the plan and technical configuration.

## 6. Refund Policy

Payments are generally non-refundable once a paid service, subscription period, credit, feature, or usage entitlement has been activated or consumed, except where:

* A refund is required under applicable law.
* Understory is unable to provide the purchased service for a material reason attributable to Understory.
* The user was charged incorrectly due to a verified billing error.
* Understory expressly approves a refund.
* A payment was duplicated or incorrectly processed.
* A transaction is determined to be unauthorized or fraudulent through the applicable investigation process.
* Another refund right applies under applicable law or the User Agreement.

A refund may be provided as a monetary refund, account credit, replacement entitlement, or another appropriate remedy where permitted by applicable law.
