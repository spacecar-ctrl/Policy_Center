# AI Safety Safeguards, Limitations and User Responsibility

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026
## AI Safety Safeguards, Limitations and User Responsibility

### 1. Understory Safety and Security Measures

Understory is designed and operated with reasonable technical, organizational, and product-level safeguards intended to reduce the risk of:

* Incorrect or misleading AI output.
* Hallucinations and fabricated information.
* Harmful or prohibited AI use.
* Prompt injection and malicious instructions.
* Unauthorized access.
* Data exposure or exfiltration.
* Unsafe AI-generated actions.
* Abuse of AI features.
* Security vulnerabilities.
* Malicious or untrusted webpage content.
* Other reasonably foreseeable risks associated with AI-assisted functionality.

Understory may use additional safeguards such as validation, filtering, access controls, provider restrictions, monitoring, rate limits, user confirmations, security testing, and other controls appropriate to the feature.

The specific technical implementation of these controls is maintained separately in the Understory Security Requirements.

### 2. No Security or AI System Can Guarantee Zero Risk

Understory applies reasonable security and safety measures, but no software, browser extension, AI model, AI provider, network, storage system, security control, or automated detection system can guarantee that every harmful, malicious, inaccurate, or unexpected event will be prevented or detected.

AI systems can produce unexpected results even when appropriate safeguards are implemented.

Security controls can also fail because of circumstances including:

* Previously unknown vulnerabilities.
* New attack techniques.
* Third-party provider failures.
* Model failures.
* Incorrect model behavior.
* Browser or operating-system changes.
* Third-party website changes.
* Network or infrastructure failures.
* Compromised third-party services.
* User actions.
* Incorrect configuration.
* Events outside Understory's reasonable control.

### 3. User Responsibility

The user remains responsible for reviewing and deciding how to use AI-generated information.

The user is responsible for:

* Reviewing AI-generated resumes.
* Reviewing AI-generated cover letters.
* Reviewing application answers.
* Verifying employment history and qualifications.
* Verifying interview-preparation information.
* Checking AI-generated recommendations.
* Reviewing information before submitting it to an employer or third party.
* Confirming actions before completing consequential actions where confirmation is provided.
* Maintaining the accuracy of their Candidate Profile.
* Using Understory only for lawful and permitted purposes.
* Protecting their own account credentials and devices.
* Not intentionally bypassing Understory security or safety controls.

The user should not assume that AI-generated information is correct merely because it was generated by Understory or an underlying AI provider.

### 4. User Actions After AI Output

AI output is assistance and not an instruction that the user must follow.

The user should independently evaluate important AI output before:

* Submitting a job application.
* Uploading a resume.
* Sending an employer or recruiter communication.
* Making a professional representation.
* Providing information to a third party.
* Making a consequential decision.

The user remains the person responsible for the final information or action they choose to submit, send, accept, or perform.

### 5. User Misuse and Deliberate Bypass

A user must not intentionally:

* Disable or bypass Understory safety controls.
* Circumvent security restrictions.
* Attempt to defeat abuse-prevention mechanisms.
* Manipulate the service to obtain prohibited assistance.
* Use malicious input to cause unauthorized actions.
* Use Understory to attack another person, service, system, or organization.
* Use Understory for prohibited activities described in this policy.

A user cannot rely on the existence of an AI feature or an AI-generated response as authorization to perform an otherwise prohibited or unlawful activity.

### 6. Third-Party AI Providers

Understory may use third-party AI models or providers.

Understory may implement provider selection, filtering, validation, data-minimization, and other safeguards, but cannot guarantee the behavior, availability, security, accuracy, or future operation of an independent third-party AI provider.

The same applies to locally operated AI models, including Ollama or other local/open-source models.

Using a local model does not guarantee that its output will be accurate, safe, secure, or free from harmful behavior.

### 7. AI Output Is Not Guaranteed to Be Safe or Accurate

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Understory does not represent or guarantee that AI-generated output will always be:

* Accurate.
* Complete.
* Current.
* Reliable.
* Appropriate.
* Error-free.
* Free from hallucinations.
* Free from unintended content.
* Suitable for a particular purpose.

Understory also does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation of AI features.

### 8. No Reliance on AI as the Sole Authority

Understory AI should not be treated as the sole authoritative source for information where independent verification is reasonably necessary.

This is particularly important for:

* Employment qualifications.
* Certifications.
* Compensation information.
* Legal matters.
* Regulatory matters.
* Health or safety matters.
* Financial matters.
* Identity-related information.
* Information that may materially affect the user's rights or opportunities.

This section describes the operational limitations and user responsibilities associated with Understory AI.

The legal allocation of warranties, disclaimers, indemnification, limitations of liability, dispute resolution, and other contractual matters is governed by the Understory User Agreement.

Nothing in this AI Safety Policy is intended to exclude or limit any liability or legal obligation that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law.

### 10. No Waiver of Understory's Own Obligations

User responsibility does not mean that Understory may intentionally:

* Misrepresent the capabilities of its AI systems.
* Ignore known security vulnerabilities.
* Make false security or accuracy claims.
* Deliberately expose user information.
* Intentionally disable reasonable safety controls.
* Ignore applicable legal obligations.

Understory will maintain reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature and risks of its AI-powered services.

### 11. Changes in AI and Security Risk

AI technology, attack techniques, third-party providers, browser platforms, and applicable requirements may change over time.

Understory may update its AI safety and security controls as reasonably necessary to address new risks, vulnerabilities, technologies, providers, or product functionality.

No update should be interpreted as a guarantee that every future risk will be identified or prevented.

### 12. Recommended User-Facing Notice

For relevant AI interfaces, Understory may display a concise notice such as:

> **AI can make mistakes.** Review AI-generated information carefully before using or submitting it. You are responsible for the information and actions you choose to submit or perform.

The notice may be shortened for smaller interfaces provided that the essential meaning is preserved.
