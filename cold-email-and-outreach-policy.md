# Understory Cold Email and Outreach Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Understory may provide a cold email and professional outreach service through supported email accounts and services such as Gmail.

The purpose of this feature is to assist users in identifying possible professional contacts and preparing or sending outreach emails based on available job, company, role, professional, or other relevant information.

The feature is an automated assistance service and does not provide verified contact information or guarantee successful communication.

## 2. Automated Email Address Identification

Understory may attempt to identify or infer a possible email address for a company, employee, recruiter, hiring contact, HR contact, professional contact, or other person.

Such email addresses may be generated or inferred using available information, including:

* Company information
* Publicly available professional information
* Name and surname
* Job title or role
* Company email-domain patterns
* Common corporate email formats
* Information provided by the user
* Previously identified email patterns
* Automated or AI-based inference

An inferred email address is only a probable address unless independently verified.

Understory does not represent that an inferred email address:

* Is correct
* Belongs to the intended person
* Is currently active
* Belongs to the intended company
* Belongs to HR, recruitment, hiring, management, or another specific function
* Is monitored by the intended person
* Has permission to receive the email
* Will successfully receive the email

## 3. No Guarantee of Recipient Identity

Cold email functionality may incorrectly identify the intended recipient.

For example, an email may be sent to:

* The wrong person
* A person with the same or similar name
* A former employee
* A different employee using the same email pattern
* A generic company mailbox
* An incorrect or inactive address
* A person whose role has changed
* A person who is not responsible for recruitment or hiring

The user is responsible for reviewing the proposed recipient where the Understory workflow provides an opportunity to review or confirm it.

## 4. Email Format and Name Inference

Understory may use common email-address formats to generate a possible email address.

Examples may include combinations based on:

* First name
* Last name
* Initials
* First name and last name
* Name abbreviations
* Company domain
* Common organizational email conventions

These patterns are not guaranteed to be used by the relevant company or person.

Understory may also make mistakes involving names, spelling, initials, titles, pronouns, or other personal or professional information.

The user must not treat automated inference as verified information.

## 5. Pronouns, Names, Titles, and Personal Information

Cold email generation may use available information to address a recipient.

Understory may incorrectly infer or use:

* Name spelling
* Preferred name
* Pronouns
* Professional title
* Job title
* Department
* Seniority
* Professional relationship
* Other recipient attributes

Understory does not guarantee that such information is accurate or current.

Users should review important recipient-specific information before sending when the workflow provides a review or confirmation option.

## 6. Email Content

Understory may generate or assist with generating short, natural-language cold emails based on information such as:

* Job descriptions
* Company information
* Professional roles
* Publicly available information
* User-provided information
* Resume information
* Career interests
* Outreach purpose

Generated content may contain factual, contextual, grammatical, personalization, or recipient-specific errors.

The user remains responsible for the final email and any claims, statements, representations, attachments, links, or other information sent through the user's email account.

## 7. Gmail and Email Account Authorization

Where supported, Understory may use the user's authorized Gmail or other supported email account to prepare or send outreach emails.

The user is responsible for:

* Authorizing the correct email account
* Maintaining account security
* Reviewing account permissions
* Ensuring the account is authorized for the intended use
* Complying with the applicable email provider's terms and policies
* Complying with applicable laws and regulations

Understory does not obtain ownership of the user's email account.

## 8. No Guarantee of Delivery

Understory does not guarantee that a cold email will:

* Be successfully sent
* Reach the intended recipient
* Avoid spam or junk folders
* Be delivered to the recipient's inbox
* Be opened
* Be read
* Receive a response
* Receive a positive response
* Result in an interview
* Result in a job opportunity
* Result in any employment or financial outcome

Email providers, receiving mail servers, spam filters, security systems, company policies, domain configuration, recipient settings, and other third parties may affect delivery.

## 9. Incorrect or Failed Email Addresses

An inferred email address may be incorrect.

An email may therefore result in:

* Delivery failure
* Bounce
* Non-delivery notification
* Automated rejection
* Spam filtering
* Delivery to an unintended recipient
* No response

A failed or incorrect email address does not by itself indicate a defect in Understory because the address may have been generated from an inference or an incomplete information set.

## 10. No Verification Representation

Unless Understory expressly identifies an address as verified through a specific verification mechanism, users must treat an automatically generated or inferred email address as unverified.

Understory must not be understood to be representing that a guessed or inferred email address is accurate merely because it is displayed, suggested, selected, or used by the cold email workflow.

The Understory AI Policy applies to AI-assisted cold email functionality.
