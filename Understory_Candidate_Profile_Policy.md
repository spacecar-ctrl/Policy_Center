# Understory Candidate Profile Policy

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 8, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

The Understory Candidate Profile is a structured collection of information provided, imported, confirmed, or maintained by a candidate for use in job-application assistance.

The profile may contain professional and career information such as:

- Professional background.
- Employment history.
- Work experience.
- Job titles.
- Responsibilities.
- Skills.
- Technical skills.
- Education.
- Certifications.
- Projects.
- Achievements.
- Professional summaries.
- Career preferences.
- Languages.
- Portfolio information.
- Other information relevant to the candidate's job applications.

The Candidate Profile is intended to help the user reuse accurate information across Understory features without repeatedly entering the same information.

---

## 2. Candidate Profile as the Source of Truth

The Candidate Profile should be treated as the primary source of candidate-provided professional information within Understory.

Information confirmed or directly provided by the candidate should be distinguishable from:

- AI-generated content.
- AI suggestions.
- AI inferences.
- Information extracted from a job description.
- Information extracted from an uploaded document.
- Information obtained from a third-party source.
- Temporary application-specific information.

AI-generated information must not automatically become a confirmed candidate fact.

Where practical, Understory should preserve the distinction between:

**Candidate fact → Confirmed by the user**

**Imported information → Obtained from an external or uploaded source**

**AI suggestion → Generated or transformed by AI**

**Unverified information → Requires candidate review**

## 4. Candidate Responsibility for Accuracy

The candidate is responsible for the accuracy of information they provide or confirm.

The candidate should not intentionally provide false information.

This includes false or misleading:

- Employment history.
- Employer names.
- Job titles.
- Employment dates.
- Responsibilities.
- Education.
- Degrees.
- Certifications.
- Licenses.
- Skills.
- Experience.
- Projects.
- Achievements.
- References.
- Compensation.
- Work authorization.
- Professional qualifications.

Understory does not independently guarantee that candidate-provided information is accurate.

---

## 5. Fabrication Prohibited

Understory must not intentionally convert unsupported information into a candidate fact.

AI must not invent:

- Employers.
- Job titles.
- Degrees.
- Certifications.
- Skills.
- Years of experience.
- Projects.
- Achievements.
- Responsibilities.
- Employment dates.
- Professional licenses.
- Awards.
- Publications.
- References.
- Other material qualifications.

If information is missing, uncertain, or ambiguous, Understory should request clarification, preserve the uncertainty, or leave the field incomplete.

The Understory AI Policy contains additional requirements regarding fabrication and deceptive use of AI.

---

## 6. AI Transformation of Candidate Information

Understory may use AI to transform candidate-provided information for legitimate job-application assistance.

Examples include:

- Improving wording.
- Correcting grammar.
- Restructuring resume content.
- Creating concise bullet points.
- Tailoring existing experience to a job description.
- Creating a professional summary based on confirmed information.
- Suggesting relevant skills already supported by the candidate's background.
- Preparing interview answers based on the candidate's actual experience.

AI transformation must not materially change the underlying facts.

For example, AI may improve:

> Managed sustainability reporting activities.

into clearer professional wording, but must not change it into an unsupported claim such as:

> Led a global sustainability transformation program across 20 countries.

unless the candidate has actually provided or confirmed that information.

---

## 7. Candidate Profile and Resume Builder

The Resume Builder may use the Candidate Profile as an information source.

The generated resume should:

- Use confirmed candidate information where available.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Preserve important dates and qualifications.
- Avoid inventing experience.
- Allow the candidate to review and modify generated content.
- Clearly treat AI-generated additions as suggestions until confirmed where appropriate.

A generated resume does not automatically become the authoritative Candidate Profile.

---

## 8. Candidate Profile and Job Matching

Understory may compare candidate information with job requirements to assist the candidate.

This may include:

- Matching skills.
- Comparing experience.
- Identifying relevant qualifications.
- Identifying potential skill gaps.
- Highlighting relevant experience.
- Preparing application suggestions.

A match or compatibility result is an AI-assisted assessment and may be incorrect or incomplete.

It must not be represented as a guaranteed hiring outcome.

---

## 9. Candidate Profile and Interview Preparation

Candidate Profile information may be used to generate interview preparation material.

Examples include:

- Questions based on work experience.
- Questions based on projects.
- Questions based on skills.
- Questions based on education.
- Suggested answers based on confirmed experience.
- Follow-up questions.
- STAR-format preparation.

AI-generated interview material must not invent experience for the candidate.

The candidate should review suggested answers and replace or correct information that does not accurately represent their experience.

---

## 10. Job-Specific Information Must Not Automatically Become Profile Information

Information obtained from a job description must not automatically be treated as information about the candidate.

For example:

A job description stating:

> Requires five years of Python experience.

must not cause Understory to add:

> Python — 5 years

to the candidate's profile unless the candidate actually confirms that information.

Job requirements describe the employer's requirements, not the candidate's qualifications.

---

## 11. Imported Resume and Document Information

Understory may allow the candidate to import information from resumes or other candidate-provided documents.

Imported information should be treated as imported information until appropriately confirmed where confirmation is required.

Extraction errors must not automatically become verified candidate facts.

The candidate should be able to correct extracted information.

---

## 12. Multiple Resumes

A candidate may use different resumes for different applications.

The Candidate Profile may act as the central source of reusable candidate information, while individual resumes may contain job-specific selections or presentation.

A tailored resume does not necessarily change the underlying Candidate Profile.

For example:

- Candidate Profile: all confirmed professional experience.
- Resume A: sustainability-focused experience.
- Resume B: consulting-focused experience.
- Resume C: financial-services-focused experience.

Understory should not delete valid profile information merely because it was omitted from a particular resume.

---

## 13. Application-Specific Information

Some information may be relevant only to a specific application.

Examples include:

- Application questions.
- Job-specific answers.
- Employer-specific preferences.
- Job-specific availability.
- Application-specific salary information.
- Application-specific statements.

Application-specific information should not automatically overwrite the Candidate Profile unless the candidate intentionally updates the profile.

---

## 14. Candidate Control

The candidate should have appropriate control over their Candidate Profile.

Where supported, the candidate should be able to:

- View profile information.
- Edit profile information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Add information.
- Remove information.
- Review AI-generated suggestions.
- Confirm suggested information.
- Decline suggested information.
- Use different information for different applications.

Understory should not intentionally prevent legitimate user corrections.

---

## 15. Candidate Profile Ownership and Rights

The candidate retains responsibility for the information they provide.

Understory does not claim ownership of the candidate's professional history merely because it is stored or processed for the purpose of providing the service.

Use, storage, processing, synchronization, retention, and deletion of Candidate Profile information are additionally governed by the Understory Privacy Policy and User Agreement.

Where third-party services process profile information, applicable third-party terms and processing conditions may also apply.

---

## 16. Sensitive Information

Candidates should avoid providing unnecessary sensitive personal information to the Candidate Profile.

Understory should collect and process only information reasonably necessary for its intended job-application assistance functions.

Understory should not intentionally infer sensitive characteristics about a candidate merely from their profile.

This includes attempting to infer sensitive characteristics from:

- Name.
- Photograph.
- Location.
- Education.
- Employment history.
- Writing style.
- Voice.
- Other indirect information.

Additional restrictions are defined in the Understory AI Policy and Security Requirements.

---

## 17. Third-Party Information

Candidates should not add another person's private or confidential information to their profile unless there is a legitimate reason and appropriate authorization.

Examples include:

- Private contact information.
- Personal identifiers.
- Confidential employment information.
- Private references.
- Credentials.
- Authentication information.

Candidate Profile data must not be used to expose or distribute another person's private information.

---

## 18. References

Where reference information is supported, the candidate is responsible for ensuring that:

- The reference is genuine.
- The information is accurate.
- The reference has been appropriately identified as a reference.
- Any required permission or consent has been obtained.

Understory must not fabricate references or recommend that users falsely identify people as professional references.

---

## 19. Career Preferences

The Candidate Profile may contain optional career preferences such as:

- Preferred roles.
- Preferred industries.
- Preferred locations.
- Work arrangement preferences.
- Employment type.
- Career interests.
- Salary expectations where voluntarily provided.
- Other job-search or application preferences.

Preferences are not qualifications.

Understory must not present a preference as an established professional fact.

---

## 20. Profile Updates

When the candidate changes information, the updated information should become the current profile information where the candidate has intentionally confirmed the change.

Historical application records may retain the information that was used at the time of an application where necessary for application history or auditability.

For example, changing a job title in the current Candidate Profile should not necessarily rewrite the historical resume or application record that was previously used.

---

## 21. AI-Generated Profile Suggestions

AI may suggest:

- Skills.
- Resume bullet improvements.
- Professional summaries.
- Relevant experience.
- Alternative wording.
- Profile improvements.

Suggestions must remain distinguishable from confirmed information until accepted or otherwise confirmed by the candidate.

AI must not silently modify confirmed candidate facts.

---

## 22. No Automatic Credential Creation

Understory must not use Candidate Profile information to fabricate:

- Degrees.
- Certifications.
- Licenses.
- Employment verification.
- Experience letters.
- Salary documents.
- Recommendation letters.
- References.
- Other evidence of qualifications.

Formatting or organizing authentic candidate-provided documents remains permitted.

---

## 23. Candidate Profile and External AI Providers

Where Candidate Profile information is sent to an external AI provider, processing must follow the applicable Understory Privacy Policy and Security Requirements.

Understory should minimize the information sent to an AI provider to what is reasonably necessary for the requested operation.

For example, generating a tailored resume for one job should not require unrelated candidate information when that information is unnecessary for the task.

The same Candidate Profile protections apply whether AI processing occurs through:

- A cloud provider.
- A third-party API.
- A local model.
- Ollama.
- An open-source model.
- A future AI provider.

---

## 24. Security

Candidate Profile information must be protected using the controls defined in the Understory Security Requirements.

Security Requirements may cover:

- Secure storage.
- Encryption.
- Access controls.
- Browser extension permissions.
- Local storage.
- Browser synchronization.
- Network transmission.
- External AI provider boundaries.
- Credential protection.
- Data-exfiltration prevention.
- Logging and monitoring.
- Backup and recovery.
- Secure deletion.

The Candidate Profile Policy defines the permitted use of the information; the Security Requirements define the technical safeguards.

---

## 25. Privacy

Collection, processing, retention, synchronization, deletion, and third-party processing of Candidate Profile information are governed by the Understory Privacy Policy.

The Candidate Profile Policy should not be interpreted as a promise that information is never processed by third parties.

Where an external provider is used, the applicable provider's processing conditions must be considered together with Understory's policies.

---

## 26. Deletion and Retention

Where Understory provides profile deletion functionality, deleting the Candidate Profile should remove or deactivate the applicable profile information according to the product's documented deletion process.

Deletion of the Candidate Profile does not necessarily mean that:

- Previously submitted applications are deleted from third-party job portals.
- Information already sent to employers is deleted.
- Information processed by third-party AI providers is immediately deleted.
- Independent backups or legally required records are immediately removed.

The Privacy Policy defines the applicable retention and deletion rules.

---

## 27. Candidate Profile Does Not Guarantee Employment

A complete or optimized Candidate Profile does not guarantee:

- Job matching.
- Interview invitations.
- Application acceptance.
- ATS acceptance.
- Recruiter responses.
- Job offers.
- Employment.
- Compensation outcomes.

The Candidate Profile is an assistance mechanism for the candidate.

---

## 28. Relationship to the AI Policy

The Understory AI Policy applies whenever AI processes, generates, transforms, evaluates, summarizes, or recommends information based on the Candidate Profile.

In particular, the AI Policy's requirements concerning:

- Hallucinations.
- Fabrication.
- Deception.
- Sensitive-attribute inference.
- Employment decisions.
- User review.
- Prohibited uses.

apply to Candidate Profile processing.

---

## 29. Relationship to the Security Requirements

The Understory Security Requirements apply to the technical handling and protection of Candidate Profile information.

This includes protection against:

- Prompt injection.
- Malicious job-page content.
- Data exfiltration.
- Unauthorized access.
- Credential theft.
- Malicious files.
- Browser abuse.
- Unauthorized automation.
- Improper external transmission.
- Other technical security threats.

---

## 30. Policy Applies to Future Candidate Profile Features

Any future Understory feature that stores, imports, processes, analyzes, generates, synchronizes, exports, or uses candidate information must follow this policy unless a stricter requirement applies.

Adding a new profile field, AI feature, job portal, resume workflow, interview workflow, storage mechanism, synchronization method, or AI provider does not remove or weaken these requirements.
