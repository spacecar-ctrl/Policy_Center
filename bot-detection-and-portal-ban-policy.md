# Understory Bot Detection and Portal Ban Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This Bot Detection and Portal Ban Policy explains how Understory handles third-party portal bot detection, CAPTCHA, anti-automation systems, rate limits, security challenges, account restrictions, suspensions, bans, and related enforcement actions.

Understory may interact with supported third-party job portals to provide features such as job compatibility or suitability assessment, resume and cover-letter assistance, application assistance, application-form completion, and supported application workflows.

Understory does not control third-party portals or their security, authentication, anti-bot, fraud-prevention, access-control, or account-enforcement systems.

Using an Understory feature that technically works on a portal does not mean that the portal permits, authorizes, endorses, or supports that use.

Users must review and comply with the applicable third-party portal's current terms, policies, restrictions, and security requirements.
