# Understory Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

Understory ("Understory", "we", "us", or "our") is a job-application assistance software product intended to help users organize job searches, assess job compatibility, prepare application materials, and automate or assist with certain application-related activities where supported.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Understory may collect, why it is collected, how it may be processed, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Understory applications, browser extensions, websites, services, and related features that link to or reference this policy.

It does not replace the privacy policies, terms, agreements, or other rules of third-party job portals, employers, AI providers, payment providers, analytics providers, or other services.

Users must separately review the applicable terms and privacy policies of every third-party portal or service they use with Understory.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, Understory may process:

### Account Information

* Name
* Email address
* Account identifiers
* Authentication information required to operate the Understory account

### Application Questions, Answers, and Application History

Understory may collect, process, and retain information associated with job applications and application workflows in order to provide, track, improve, and secure the service.

This may include:

* Questions displayed during a job application
* Question text
* Question type, such as text field, number field, checkbox, radio button, dropdown, date field, or other input type
* Available answer options
* User-selected answers
* User-entered answers
* AI-generated or AI-assisted suggested answers
* Corrected or modified answers
* Application-form fields
* Information associated with the application question
* Job or employer associated with the question
* Portal associated with the application
* Application status
* Application date and time
* Application workflow information
* Documents or information associated with the application
* Information necessary to identify or track an application
* Other information reasonably necessary to maintain application history

### Purpose of Collecting Application Questions and Answers

Understory may use application-question and answer information for purposes including:

* Completing and tracking job applications
* Maintaining application history
* Preventing duplicate or unintended applications
* Allowing users to review previous applications
* Improving application workflows
* Identifying recurring application questions
* Building and maintaining a question bank
* Identifying similar or duplicate questions
* Improving question classification and field detection
* Improving AI-assisted answer suggestions
* Improving application automation
* Testing and improving Understory features
* Product analytics and service improvement
* Detecting errors and application failures
* Security, fraud, and abuse prevention
* Troubleshooting
* Training or improving applicable models, systems, classifiers, or automation components, where permitted and appropriately configured
* Other purposes described in this Privacy Policy

Where reasonably practical, Understory may use techniques such as aggregation, de-identification, anonymization, filtering, or removal of unnecessary personal information when using application-question data for analytics, product improvement, training, testing, or similar purposes.

However, users should understand that application questions may contain personal, professional, employment, demographic, financial, or other information depending on the particular job application.

Users should review important application information before submitting it to a third-party portal.

Understory may retain application questions, answers, application history, and related information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention may be necessary to:

* Maintain application history

* Protect the rights, security, and property of Understory, users, or others

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Some information may be retained for a defined period, while other information may be retained until it is no longer reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

Deletion requests may be subject to:

### Question-Bank and Training Data

Understory may use application questions and related information to develop and improve its question bank, question matching, classification, automation, AI-assisted answering, and related systems.

Where technically and reasonably practical, Understory may separate reusable question structure from unnecessary personal information before using information for these purposes.

For example, Understory may identify that a particular application contains a question and preserve information about the question's structure, type, available options, or generalized form without unnecessarily retaining the user's specific personal answer.

However, not all application questions can be separated completely from the information provided with them. Users should therefore understand that information submitted through an application workflow may be processed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Any use of application information for AI or model-related purposes is also subject to the `ai-policy.md` and applicable third-party AI provider terms where a third-party AI service is involved.

### Third-Party Portal Login and Passwords

Understory does **not collect, request, or store** users' passwords for third-party job portals.

Understory does not require users to provide their LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, or other third-party portal passwords to Understory.

Users must log in directly to the applicable third-party portal through their browser. Understory's portal-related features may rely on the user's existing authenticated browser session.

Understory does not store third-party portal passwords in its servers, databases, application storage, analytics systems, or AI processing systems.

Understory also does not intentionally send third-party portal passwords to AI providers, analytics providers, or other Understory service providers.

The third-party portal independently controls its authentication credentials, login sessions, cookies, authentication tokens, MFA, CAPTCHA, verification procedures, and account-security mechanisms.

If a user is not logged in to the applicable portal, features that require an authenticated session may not function until the user completes the portal's own login or verification process.

Users should review the applicable third-party portal's current Terms of Service, User Agreement, Privacy Policy, and security requirements before using Understory with that portal.
