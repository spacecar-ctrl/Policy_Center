# Understory Login and Password Safety Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

Understory is designed to assist users with job-search and job-application workflows while minimizing the collection and handling of third-party account credentials.

## 1. Third-Party Portal Passwords

Understory does **not collect, request, or store** users' passwords for third-party job portals.

Understory does not require users to provide their LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, or other third-party portal passwords to Understory.

Users should never provide their third-party portal password to Understory, an Understory support representative, an AI service, or another person.

## 2. Browser-Based Login

Users must log in directly to the applicable third-party job portal through their browser.

Understory's portal-related features may rely on the user's existing authenticated browser session.

For example, a user may first log in directly to a supported job portal in their browser. Once the user has established an authenticated session, Understory may operate within the browser environment where the user is already logged in, subject to the technical capabilities and restrictions of the applicable portal.

Understory does not independently create the user's third-party portal account or password.

## 3. Understory Does Not Store Portal Passwords

Understory does not store third-party portal passwords in:

* Understory servers.
* Understory databases.
* Extension application storage.
* Understory analytics systems.
* Understory logging systems.
* Understory AI systems.
* Cloud AI services used by Understory.
* Other Understory-controlled storage.

Understory also does not intentionally send third-party portal passwords to AI providers, analytics providers, payment providers, or other Understory service providers.

## 4. Existing Browser Sessions

An authenticated browser session may allow Understory to access features of a third-party portal that are available to the user while they are logged in.

The third-party portal controls the authentication session.

Depending on the portal and browser, an authenticated session may involve information such as:

* Cookies.
* Session identifiers.
* Authentication tokens.
* Login state.
* MFA state.
* Other browser authentication mechanisms.

Understory does not replace or control the third-party portal's authentication system.

## 5. Login Required for Portal Features

Understory may not function for certain portal-related features if the user is not logged in to the applicable portal.

If login is required, the user must complete the login process directly through the applicable portal.

This may include:

* Username or email entry.
* Password entry.
* Multi-factor authentication.
* CAPTCHA.
* Email or phone verification.
* Identity verification.
* Other security checks required by the portal.

Understory does not guarantee that every portal will provide or permit access to its functionality through an authenticated browser session.

## 6. Authentication and Security Controls

Third-party portals independently control:

* Passwords.
* Authentication.
* Login sessions.
* Cookies.
* Authentication tokens.
* Multi-factor authentication.
* CAPTCHA.
* Identity verification.
* Security challenges.
* Account restrictions.
* Account suspensions.
* Account recovery.

Understory does not guarantee that a third-party portal will maintain a user's session or permit Understory's features to operate.

## 7. AI and Credential Safety

Third-party portal passwords, authentication tokens, session credentials, and similar authentication secrets must not be intentionally submitted to an Understory AI feature.

Understory's AI features are intended to process information required for the requested task, such as job descriptions, resumes, application questions, and other permitted application information.

Understory does not intentionally send third-party portal passwords or authentication credentials to cloud AI providers.

Users must not intentionally provide passwords, authentication tokens, session cookies, API keys, or other confidential credentials as input to an AI feature.

## 8. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for protecting their own:

* Devices.
* Browser profiles.
* Portal accounts.
* Passwords.
* Authentication sessions.
* MFA credentials.
* Recovery information.

Users should:

* Use strong and unique passwords.
* Enable MFA where supported.
* Keep their browser and operating system updated.
* Avoid sharing authenticated browser profiles.
* Avoid entering passwords into unknown websites or extensions.
* Log out of third-party portals when appropriate.
* Review active sessions and account-security settings periodically.
* Disable or remove Understory when they no longer want to use it.

## 9. Understory Support

Understory support personnel will not request a user's third-party portal password.

Users should not send passwords, session cookies, authentication tokens, API keys, or other authentication secrets through support requests, screenshots, logs, email, chat, or other communication channels.

If troubleshooting requires account information, users should provide only the minimum information necessary and should remove or redact credentials and authentication secrets.

## 10. Security Incidents

If a user believes that their third-party portal credentials or account session may have been compromised, the user should immediately follow the affected portal's security procedures.

Depending on the situation, this may include:

* Changing the portal password.
* Revoking active sessions.
* Enabling or resetting MFA.
* Reviewing account activity.
* Contacting the portal's security or support team.

Users may notify Understory if they believe an Understory security issue may have contributed to an incident so that Understory can investigate the reported issue.

Understory is **not responsible for security incidents, account compromises, credential exposure, unauthorized access, account restrictions, or other losses arising from a third-party portal, the user's device, browser, browser session, network, credentials, user actions, or other circumstances outside Understory's reasonable control, except where liability cannot be excluded or limited under applicable law.

Understory does not control the security infrastructure, authentication systems, account protection, or security practices of third-party job portals.

Users remain responsible for securing their devices, browser sessions, third-party accounts, passwords, and authentication credentials.

## 11. Third-Party Portal Policies

Understory is independent from third-party employment portals.

Understory is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected with LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, or other third-party portals unless expressly stated otherwise.

The fact that Understory can technically operate with a particular portal does not mean that the portal permits or endorses Understory.

Users are responsible for reviewing the applicable portal's current:

* Terms of Service.
* User Agreement.
* Privacy Policy.
* Automation rules.
* Security requirements.
* Account restrictions.
* Other applicable policies.

Users must comply with the applicable portal's requirements.

## 12. Portal Security and Account Restrictions

A third-party portal may detect automated, unusual, or unsupported activity and may require additional verification or restrict an account.

Understory does not guarantee that using the service will prevent:

* CAPTCHA challenges.
* Security checks.
* Login interruptions.
* Session expiration.
* Account restrictions.
* Temporary suspension.
* Permanent account suspension.
* Other portal enforcement actions.

Users should follow the applicable portal's instructions if an account-security or verification event occurs.

## 13. No Password Recovery Through Understory

Understory does not manage or recover third-party portal passwords.

Password resets, account recovery, MFA recovery, identity verification, and related account-security processes must be completed through the applicable third-party portal.

## 14. No Absolute Security Guarantee

Understory uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the service and information processed through it.

However, no software, browser extension, device, network, or internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Third-party portals independently control their own authentication and security infrastructure.
